## 📌 Project Overview
This repository documents the end-to-end execution, empirical data, analysis scripts, and deliverables for the academic research project: **"The Relationship Between AI Literacy and University Students’ Verification of AI-Generated Academic Information"**.

Generative artificial intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT) are increasingly integrated into undergraduate academic workflows for drafting, summarization, and literature searching. However, large language models frequently produce inaccurate content, fabrications, and hallucinated citations. This study examines the relationship between students' **AI literacy**—measured across four dimensions: **awareness, usage, evaluation, and ethics**—and their actual **verification behavior** regarding AI-generated academic information.

The study employs a quantitative, cross-sectional correlational survey design targeting undergraduate students at Addis Ababa University who use generative AI in their academic work.

## 🎯 Research Questions & Objectives

### Research Questions
1. **RQ1:** What is the relationship between university students’ AI literacy (overall and in its four dimensions of awareness, usage, evaluation, and ethics) and their verification of AI-generated academic information?
2. **RQ2:** Do students’ verification practices differ across levels of AI literacy (low, moderate, high), and which AI-literacy dimension is the strongest predictor of verification after accounting for year of study, field of study, and frequency of AI use?

### Objectives
- Measure baseline levels of AI literacy and verification practices among university students.
- Determine the strength and direction of the association between AI literacy dimensions and information verification.
- Compare verification practices across low, moderate, and high AI literacy groups.
- Identify which AI-literacy dimension best predicts verification behavior using multiple regression modeling.
- Provide evidence-based recommendations for lecturers and higher-education institutions on AI literacy curricula and verification guidelines.

## 🛠️ Methodology & Software Tools
- **Research Design:** Quantitative cross-sectional, correlational survey design
- **Sample Size:** $N = 150$ undergraduate students (Cochran formula, 95% CI, $\pm 8\%$ margin of error)
- **Data Collection:** Structured questionnaire evaluating background, AI literacy knowledge, and verification practices
- **Statistical Software:** **JASP** (Cronbach’s alpha reliability, descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, and multiple regression analysis)
- **Reference Standard:** APA 7th Edition
